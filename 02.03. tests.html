How to use it

  • ✅Flip the flash-cards until every front side triggers the back side from memory — the “what”.
  • ✅Take the quiz to practise the judgement — the “why”. An MCQ (Multiple-Choice Question) has one right answer among four; an MSQ (Multiple-Select Question) has six options and shows, in round brackets next to the question, how many right answers are expected — no point unless you find all of them and nothing else.
  • ✅Every question has a hint, an immediate answer and the reason under each option. Finish the quiz to see your breakdown by topic and the questions to work on; then repeat it with “Shuffle: on”.
  • ✅Everything here comes from the Lab 02 documents and the visual walkthrough — re-read them whenever a rationale surprises you.
