Design before you build: measure it, structure it, name its patterns, choose its framework.

This Study Hub holds the terms and the reasoning of Class 6 — the design model and the design concepts, cohesion and coupling with the numbers that measure them, architectural styles and the ADR (Architecture Decision Record) that records a choice, the SOLID (Single responsibility, Open–closed, Liskov substitution, Interface segregation, Dependency inversion) principles with DRY (Don't Repeat Yourself), KISS (Keep It Simple, Stupid) and YAGNI (You Aren't Gonna Need It), nine design patterns of the GoF (Gang of Four — Gamma, Helm, Johnson and Vlissides), and frameworks, component-level design and sequence diagrams. Use the tabs above to switch between memorising the terms and testing yourself on the reasoning.

How to use it

  • ✅Flip the flash-cards until every front side brings back the back side from memory — the “what”.
  • ✅Take the quiz to practise the reasoning — the “why”. A MCQ (multiple-choice question — one correct answer) has one right answer among four options; a MSQ (multiple-select question — several correct answers) has six options and shows, in round brackets next to the question, how many right answers are expected. A multiple-select question earns its point only when you find all the right answers and nothing else.
  • ✅Every question has a hint, an immediate answer, a button that reveals the answer, and a note under every option saying why it is right or wrong. Finish the quiz to see your breakdown by lesson and the questions to work on; then repeat it with “Shuffle: on”.
  • ✅Everything here comes from the Class 6 module and its lecture: re-read the lesson whenever a note surprises you, and run the five demo programs step by step to see the ideas move.
