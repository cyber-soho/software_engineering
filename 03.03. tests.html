How to use it

  • ✅Flip the flash-cards until every front side brings back the back side from memory — the “what”.
  • ✅Take the quiz to practise the reasoning — the “why”. A MCQ (Multiple-Choice Question) has one right answer among four options; a MSQ (Multiple-Select Question) has six options and shows, in round brackets next to the question, how many right answers are expected. A multiple-select question earns its point only when you find all the right answers and nothing else.
  • ✅Every question has a hint, an immediate answer, a button that reveals the answer, and a note under every option saying why it is right or wrong. Finish the quiz to see your breakdown by lesson and the questions to work on; then repeat it with “Shuffle: on”.
  • ✅Everything here comes from the Class 3 module and its lecture: re-read the lesson whenever a note surprises you, and run the five demos to see the ideas move.
